--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Topic page for one special interest. Two states, both driven from the same markup: at rest the timeline runs full width and every detail panel is hidden selected the timeline narrows to a left column and the chosen entry's panel shows on the right, the way a mail client shows a message Where an entry's content lives depends on the entry: `post:` the post is the source of truth. Its title, URL and opening paragraph are pulled at build time, so rewriting the post updates this page. The entry supplies only `why` — where the post sits in this story, which is the one thing the post cannot know about itself. anything no post exists behind it, so this page is the only place on else the site that says what happened. `what` and `note` carry it. A post may appear in several interests; nothing here is exclusive, and each interest writes its own `why`. Where that happens the detail panel links across, so the connection is navigable rather than just true. The panels are all rendered into the page up front, so selecting one is a class toggle rather than a fetch — which keeps it working under the site's `script-src 'self'` CSP and under `baseurl`, and keeps every entry in the HTML for search engines and for readers with JavaScript off.
